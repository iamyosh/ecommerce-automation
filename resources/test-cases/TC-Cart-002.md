**Test Case ID**: TC-Cart-002

**Test Case Title**: Verify user can add multiple products to cart

**Preconditions**
* User must be in Products page
* Products must be available

**Test Steps**
* Open website Homepage
* Navigate to Products page
* Hover over a product 1
* Click "Add to Cart" button
* Hover over product 2
* Click "Add to Cart" button
* Navigate to Cart page

**Test Data**
* Product 1 name: Blue Top
* Product 2 name: Men Tshirt

**Expected Result**
* Selected products should appear in the cart with correct product details

**Actual Result**
* Product 1 and Product 2 both successfully added to cart with correct details

* Pass
